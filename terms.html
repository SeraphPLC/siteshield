Subscription terms

SiteShield monthly service terms.

Business and Pro are recurring monthly subscriptions billed through Stripe at the price shown at checkout. The subscription renews monthly until canceled.

Activation and authorization

Paid service activation begins after the subscription is matched to the onboarding request and the enrolled website, ownership or management authority, and monitoring scope are verified. Payment purchases monitoring service only. It does not itself authorize active scanning, credential use, exploitation, state changes, or testing outside a separately verified and explicitly authorized scope.

Monitoring cadence

Business: after activation, the enrolled public website receives scheduled passive monitoring at least once every 7 days, prioritized findings, remediation guidance, and evidence-backed rechecks of reported fixes within the authorized scope.

Pro: after activation, the enrolled public website receives scheduled passive monitoring at least once every 24 hours, plus expanded authorized coverage for higher-complexity website, API, or ecommerce surfaces agreed during onboarding, prioritized findings, remediation guidance, and evidence-backed rechecks.

Event-driven or manual rechecks may occur sooner when a material change or reported fix needs verification. Active testing is performed only when the separate authorization requirements for that exact scope are satisfied.

Service boundaries

SiteShield provides security monitoring, evidence-backed findings, remediation guidance, and verification. It does not guarantee that a website is secure, vulnerability-free, compliant, continuously available, or protected against every threat. A scheduled check is evidence about the observations made within that check's scope; it is not a certification.

If the enrolled site, scope, or authorization state prevents a scheduled check from being performed safely, SiteShield may pause the affected check and request updated information rather than testing outside the verified boundary.

Cancellation and refunds

You may request cancellation through the SiteShield contact form. Cancellation stops future renewals after it is processed; access already paid for continues through the end of the current billing period unless otherwise stated.

Refund requests are reviewed individually, subject to applicable law and any payment already earned for services delivered. Nothing on this page limits rights that cannot legally be waived.

Changes to a paid plan

Material changes to the price or core monitoring cadence of a paid plan will not silently create a higher recurring charge. Material service changes will be disclosed before they are applied to a renewed paid plan when notice is reasonably required.
