Subscription terms

SiteShield monthly service terms.

Business and Pro are recurring monthly subscriptions billed through Stripe at the price shown at checkout. The subscription renews monthly until canceled.

You may request cancellation through the SiteShield contact form. Cancellation stops future renewals after it is processed; access already paid for continues through the end of the current billing period unless otherwise stated.

Refund requests are reviewed individually, subject to applicable law and any payment already earned for services delivered. Nothing on this page limits rights that cannot legally be waived.

Payment purchases monitoring service only. It does not itself authorize active scanning, credential use, exploitation, state changes, or testing outside a separately verified and explicitly authorized scope.

SiteShield provides security monitoring, evidence-backed findings, remediation guidance, and verification. It does not guarantee that a website is secure, vulnerability-free, compliant, or protected against every threat.

Service availability, monitoring cadence, and specific coverage may change as the product evolves, but material changes to a paid plan will not silently create a higher recurring charge without customer authorization.
