Privacy

Collect less. Keep evidence explicit.

SiteShield limits public intake to information needed to provide, support, and secure the service. Payment details stay with the payment processor rather than the public website.

Information you submit

Public forms may collect a website URL, contact email, business name, selected service or plan, authorization confirmation, and notes you choose to provide. Do not submit passwords, API keys, customer records, private keys, or other secrets.

SiteShield uses form information to respond to requests, reconcile paid onboarding, verify scope and authorization, provide monitoring and support, investigate service or security issues, and maintain necessary business records.

Payments

Subscription checkout is processed by Stripe. SiteShield does not receive or store full payment-card numbers or card security codes through this website. Payment does not authorize active testing of a website.

Hosting and service providers

The public site and intake forms use hosted infrastructure, including Netlify for website hosting/form handling and Stripe for subscription checkout. Those providers process data under their own privacy and security terms.

Retention and deletion

Intake, onboarding, support, billing-reference, authorization, and security-evidence records are retained only as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, resolve support or billing matters, preserve necessary security/audit history, and meet legal or accounting obligations. Records that are no longer needed are deleted or de-identified where reasonably practicable.

Your requests

You may use the SiteShield contact form to ask about personal information associated with your request, request a correction, or request deletion where applicable. We may need to verify the requester before changing or deleting records.

Data boundaries

Submitting a form or purchasing a subscription does not authorize credential use, exploitation, state changes, access to non-public systems, or active testing. Additional production-data handling or broader testing requires a separate documented scope and authorization.

SiteShield does not sell information submitted through its public intake and onboarding forms.
