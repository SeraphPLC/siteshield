Privacy

Collect less. Keep evidence explicit.

This public prototype does not include live customer accounts, payment processing, production credential storage, or unrestricted active scanning.

Current data boundary

The public intake forms accept only the website URL, contact email, selected service interest, and optional notes that you choose to submit. Do not submit passwords, API keys, customer records, or other secrets. SiteShield self-monitoring remains bounded to its own explicitly authorized scope.

Form submissions are processed by our hosting provider for intake and follow-up. Submitting a form does not authorize active testing, credential use, exploitation, state changes, or access to non-public systems. Additional production-data handling requires a separate documented agreement.
