Privacy

Collect less. Keep evidence explicit.

This public prototype does not include live customer accounts, payment processing, production credential storage, or unrestricted active scanning.

Current data boundary

The current public surface does not accept customer production data or credentials. SiteShield self-monitoring is bounded to its own explicitly authorized scope.

Any future live service must document retention, access, deletion, subprocessors, and incident-handling practices before customer data is accepted.
