What is AI Continuity Engineering (ACE)?

AI Continuity Engineering (ACE) is our shorthand for the practice of carrying mission state, evidence, decisions, unresolved questions, and authority boundaries across changes in AI worker, chat, model, machine, or human operator. We are describing our approach, not claiming ACE is an industry standard.
